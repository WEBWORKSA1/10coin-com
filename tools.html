    Calculator FAQ

    How is coin melt value calculated?

    Melt value = pure metal content (troy ounces) × spot price × quantity. For example a pre-1965 US quarter contains 0.18084 troy oz of silver.

    Why is my dealer offering less than melt value?

    Dealers need a margin to cover refining, storage and risk. Offers commonly range below melt for junk silver and above melt for popular bullion coins. Compare several quotes.

    How far back does the “If I had invested” tool go?

    The free data source typically returns daily prices for about the past year. For longer histories, check a full-featured charting site.
