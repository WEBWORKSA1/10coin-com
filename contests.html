Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  • Open to individuals 18+ (or age of majority) where such contests are legal. Void where prohibited. Employees and immediate families of 10Coin and sponsors are not eligible.
  • One entry per person per contest per month. Entries must be original and submitted before the countdown ends (00:00 local time on the 1st).
  • Skill-based judging by the 10Coin editorial team (Spotter, Story) or closest numerical result (Prediction). Judges’ decisions are final.
  • Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days; otherwise an alternate may be selected. Prizes are non-transferable; cash alternatives are at the sponsor’s discretion. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  • By entering you grant 10Coin a non-exclusive licence to publish your entry with credit. We handle personal data under our Privacy Policy.
  • 10Coin may modify or cancel a contest if fraud, technical failure or other factors impair its integrity. Full rules for each round may be published with the announcement.
