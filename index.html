FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I find out what my coin is worth?

Identify the country, denomination, year and mint mark, then estimate its condition (grade). Check our coin value guide for key dates and errors, use the melt calculator for silver or gold coins, and request a free valuation for anything that looks special.

Is the 10Coin valuation really free?

Yes. Asking costs nothing and there is no obligation to sell. If you choose to sell or grade a coin, any partner fees are disclosed up front.

Where does your market data come from?

Crypto prices and global stats come from the CoinGecko public API; the sentiment gauge comes from Alternative.me. Gold and silver references are derived from metal-backed tokens and are indicative only.

Is 10Coin a cryptocurrency or token?

No. 10Coin.com is an independent information website. We do not issue, sell or promote any token, coin offering or rewards program. See our trademark notice.

Should I clean my old coins before selling?

No. Cleaning almost always reduces collector value — sometimes by 50% or more. Handle coins by the edges and keep them as found.
