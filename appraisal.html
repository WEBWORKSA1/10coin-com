Valuation FAQ

How much does a valuation cost?

Nothing. The initial estimate is free and there is no obligation. If you later choose paid grading or an auction, costs are explained before you commit.

Do I need photos?

Clear front and back photos in daylight make the estimate faster and more accurate, but you can submit first and send photos later.

Will you buy my coins?

If you want to sell, we can introduce you to reputable dealers or auction houses matched to your items and location, so you can compare offers.

Can you help with inherited collections?

Yes — estates are one of the most common requests. We’ll help you sort the valuable pieces from face-value coins and plan next steps.
