Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What we collect

  • Information you give us through forms (valuation requests, newsletter, contest entries, applications, pledges, inquiries): e.g. name, email, phone, country and the details you choose to share.
  • Automatically: standard technical data (browser, device, pages viewed) through analytics and advertising cookies, if you consent.
  • Stored only in your browser: watchlist, price alerts, theme and cookie preferences (localStorage). We cannot see these.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and deliver valuations, newsletters and contest results.
  • With your consent, to introduce you to a vetted partner (e.g. dealer, auction house, grading or tax professional) relevant to your request.
  • To improve the site, prevent abuse and measure performance.
  • To show advertising, including personalised ads where permitted.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Form submissions are transmitted by a secure form-delivery service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. Market data is loaded from CoinGecko and Alternative.me. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode.

Google AdSense & cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See also how Google uses information from sites that use its services and our Cookie Policy.

Retention

We keep inquiry and lead data for up to 24 months, newsletter data until you unsubscribe, and applications for up to 12 months, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, India DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Use the contact form with topic “Privacy / data request”.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and contests are limited to adults.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
