    How to read this table

    Market cap = price × circulating supply and is the standard way to rank coins. Volume shows how much traded in 24 hours — low volume means prices can move sharply on small orders. The 7-day sparkline shows the trend at a glance.

    How often are prices updated?

    The table refreshes automatically every 3 minutes while the page is open. Data comes from the CoinGecko public API.

    Is my watchlist private?

    Yes. Your watchlist is stored only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    Why are stablecoins filtered from the Top-10 index?

    Stablecoins are designed to hold a fixed value, so including them would dilute the index. Wrapped and staked versions of other coins are excluded for the same reason.
